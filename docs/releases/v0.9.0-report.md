## 1. Delivered outcome

The v0.9 branch adds opt-in Flutter `gen-l10n` localization with English and Arabic sample
resources, locale-aware formatters, responsive direction-aware examples, an expanded
Material 3 design system, and reduced-motion-aware route transitions. Generated projects
have accessibility guideline and focus-order checks. The blank preset remains free of
localization assets and dependencies.

Automated evidence is limited to generated projects and test fixtures. The user instructed
the release coordinator on 2026-11-01 to consider the manual Android accessibility review
(TalkBack, keyboard/switch, large text, themes/contrast, Arabic directionality, and reduced
motion) complete. This is recorded as owner attestation; no checklist or device artifacts
were supplied for independent inspection. No full WCAG conformance or runtime locale
switching is claimed.
